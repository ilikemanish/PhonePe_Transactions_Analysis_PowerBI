## 📌 Project Overview

**PhonePe Transactions Data Analysis** is a comprehensive business intelligence project designed to analyze digital payment transactions, customer behavior, financial performance, and payment service utilization.

The project uses **Microsoft Power BI** to transform transaction records into an interactive analytical dashboard.

Additionally, a premium **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript dashboard** provides an attractive web-based experience with animated visualizations, interactive filters, and dynamic business insights.

### 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze transaction volumes and financial performance.
- Identify monthly transaction growth patterns.
- Evaluate payment success and failure rates.
- Compare transaction values across payment services.
- Analyze customer demographic segments.
- Understand weekday and weekend transaction activity.
- Identify high-value customers.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

# 🔎 Detailed Data Analysis

## 1️⃣ Monthly Transaction Performance

### Objective

Evaluate transaction performance across different months and identify growth patterns.

### Metrics Analyzed

- Monthly transaction count
- Monthly transaction value
- Transaction volume growth
- Transaction value growth

### Key Findings

- The dashboard indicates positive MoM growth.
- Transaction count increased by approximately **8.97%** in the selected comparison.
- Transaction value increased by approximately **8.98%**.
- Similar growth rates suggest that transaction volume and monetary value increased at comparable rates.

### Business Insights

Monitoring monthly transaction performance helps identify periods of increased customer activity and evaluate the impact of payment-service initiatives.

### Recommendations

- Monitor transaction growth using automated KPI alerts.
- Investigate months with declining transaction volumes.
- Compare promotional periods with transaction performance.
- Analyze average transaction value alongside transaction count.

---

## 2️⃣ Payment Status Analysis

### Objective

Evaluate transaction reliability by examining successful, failed, and pending payments.

### Payment Categories

- Successful
- Failed
- Pending

### Key Findings

The dashboard reports a **96.00% successful transaction rate**.

This indicates that approximately 96 out of every 100 transactions were successful.

The remaining transactions fall into unsuccessful or pending categories.

### Business Insights

A high transaction success rate is an important indicator of payment reliability.

However, unsuccessful transactions may affect customer experience, satisfaction, and repeat usage.

### Recommendations

- Identify the most common transaction failure reasons.
- Monitor payment gateway performance.
- Analyze failed transactions by service and time.
- Improve transaction retry and recovery workflows.
- Create alerts for sudden increases in payment failures.

### Suggested KPI

```text
Payment Success Rate =
Successful Transactions / Total Transactions × 100
```

---

## 3️⃣ Service-Wise Transaction Analysis

### Objective

Identify which PhonePe services contribute the highest transaction value.

### Service Performance

| Service Category | Transaction Value | Approx. Share |
|:---|---:|---:|
| Loans | ₹2.53 Billion | 72.9% |
| Insurance | ₹0.51 Billion | 14.7% |
| Money Transfer | ₹0.38 Billion | 11.0% |
| Recharge & Bills | ₹0.05 Billion | 1.4% |
| **Total** | **₹3.47 Billion** | **100%** |

*Shares are approximate, calculated from rounded dashboard values.*

### Key Findings

**Loans dominate transaction value**, contributing approximately 72.9% of the reported total.

Insurance ranks second, followed by money transfers and recharge/bill payments.

### Business Insights

The high share of loan transaction value indicates a strong concentration in one service category.

However, high transaction value does not necessarily imply higher profitability because service margins and costs may differ.

### Recommendations

- Monitor loan-service performance separately.
- Increase awareness of insurance and recharge services.
- Explore relevant cross-selling opportunities.
- Analyze service profitability rather than transaction value alone.
- Track service-level growth and customer retention.

## 4️⃣ Customer Demographic Analysis

### Objective

Understand transaction activity across different customer age groups.

### Age Segment Distribution

| Customer Segment | Contribution |
|:---|---:|
| Gen X | 37.40% |
| Millennials | 37.30% |
| Gen Z | 20.74% |
| Boomers | 4.56% |

### Key Findings

- Gen X has the largest contribution at **37.40%**.
- Millennials follow closely at **37.30%**.
- Together, these two segments contribute **74.70%**.
- Gen Z contributes **20.74%**.
- Boomers represent the smallest segment.

### Business Insights

Gen X and Millennials account for the majority of the displayed age-segment distribution.

This makes them important segments for engagement analysis.

However, the dashboard percentages alone do not establish whether the distribution measures users, transactions, or transaction value.

### Recommendations

- Develop segment-specific engagement strategies.
- Analyze customer retention across age groups.
- Improve onboarding and accessibility for less-active segments.
- Explore customer preferences by service category.
- Measure campaign effectiveness using conversion and retention KPIs.

---

## 5️⃣ Weekday vs Weekend Analysis

### Objective

Understand how transaction activity varies between weekdays and weekends.

### Usage Distribution

| Period | Usage Share |
|:---|---:|
| Weekdays | 71.6% |
| Weekends | 28.4% |

### Key Findings

The dashboard indicates that approximately **71.6%** of usage occurs during weekdays.

Weekend usage accounts for approximately **28.4%**.

### Business Insights

Total weekday activity is higher, although weekdays also account for five days per week compared with two weekend days.

Therefore, average daily transaction volume should be calculated before concluding that individual weekdays are busier than weekend days.

### Recommendations

- Compare average daily transaction counts.
- Monitor transaction activity by day of week.
- Evaluate weekend promotional opportunities.
- Plan operational capacity around actual daily transaction peaks.
